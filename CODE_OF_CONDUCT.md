# Code of Conduct

This is a short code of conduct in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1 (CC BY 4.0). It is adapted and condensed, not a verbatim copy.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project without harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and kind. Assume good intent, and ask when unsure.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologize, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or attention, or sexual advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or private.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address, without permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would call inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a person for behavior they judge inappropriate. They will explain the reason when it is safe to do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer by email at **De-real-iManuel@hotmail.com**, or by direct message to X/Twitter **@De_real_Manuel**.

Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers who are involved in a report must step aside from deciding it.
